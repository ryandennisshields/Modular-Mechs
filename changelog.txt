- Notable Changes
> !!!Quick Heal binding is now disabled inside the Mech!!!
> To compensate for the change, Mech now always has a base +100 life bonus regardless of parts
/ The reason behind disabling the Quick Heal bind is to further push the Mech as a "temporary power-up with unique downsides", to make the usage of the Mech more strategic instead of just being free to utilise as extra health/damage (remember the Mech will save you from death!)
> Mechs (with a compatible Booster) can now dash in any direction instead of just left or right (however, dashing upwards eats into your flight time!)
> A tip has been added to the Mechanist to notify players of the above dash change

- Changes, fixes and improvements
> Swapped around the default "Mech Dash" and "Mech Activate Module" bindings
> Right-click functionality of hotbar items is now disabled while in Mech
> Mana cost Mech weapons will now work with automatic Mana restoration accessories (e.g. Mana Flower)
> Fixed upgrade damage bonuses staying if Calamity Mod is disabled after already upgrading the Mech
> Right-click equipping Mech Parts now warns for invalid Mech Parts
> Metal pipe sound fixed :)
